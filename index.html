Public on-chain

  • One entry from a public wallet, in a fixed size, batched per epoch
  • The Omnibus Adapter's total position per vault, for reserve checks
  • Exits you choose to make, in fixed sizes after a delay

Hidden by the pool

  • Who holds what, and how much
  • Every action inside the pool: earn, swap, borrow, transfer
  • Any link between your entry, your actions and an exit

Provable, to whom you choose

  • A note is yours and what it holds (disclosure key)
  • Where an asset came from, from entry to today
  • Your funds come from screened deposits

Simulated in this build

  • Your key is derived here; live notes go to your Orbinum Hub privacy address
  • Notes, Merkle tree and nullifiers live in this browser
  • Proofs come from a mock prover that checks every circuit constraint
  • The relayer is local; no transaction is sent

Routes and share prices are live. No funds move.
